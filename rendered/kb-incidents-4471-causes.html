#4471 says one issue, one fix queued. Its reports say otherwise. The desk merged them by subject line (decision 0001), and a subject line like “Wrong day on the invite” fits three different bugs.

The queued fix is for the parser. It does not fix the form hint or the one-day move. Those reports stay open after #4471 closes.

The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant whose locale writes the day first gets the day and month swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. Any channel.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint tells them and the date swaps. Only the form channel, only booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it.
  • one-day move: the date moves by one day, not by month. Monday instead of Tuesday, an invite before its booking, Lisbon and Toronto on different days, “only June”. That reads as a time zone or summer-time bug. The desk had these flagged in its notes and still counted them as parser.

How the reports are placed

By the tenant, not by the subject and not by intake’s tag. Intake tags hint when the reporter’s browser is en-US; that is a guess about the person, not the form. The tenant directory says which locale and which booking form each tenant runs, and src/lib/kb/problems.ts places each report from that and the desk’s notes. The table above runs it on every build, so new reports land in the right row without anyone editing this page.

Lessons

  • A shared subject is not a shared cause. Merge on what broke, not on what people wrote.
  • A note that says “these are different” belongs in the count, not in a footnote under it.
